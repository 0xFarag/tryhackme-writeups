## Included completion matches

UltraTech, Ignite, Basic Pentesting, Simple CTF, LazyAdmin, TomGhost, Anonymous and RootMe were matched by room name against the profile and the corresponding study entry. Minor differences in capitalisation and spacing were normalised.

Other rooms are not represented as completed solely because a note exists. In particular, an unfinished note, a copied reference or a reconnaissance fragment is insufficient evidence for a full completion claim. Dates associated with note files are not used as solve dates.

## Editorial treatment

The room notes were used to choose the topics to discuss. Third-party walkthrough prose, shell transcripts, screenshots, credentials, flags, hidden endpoints, exact challenge paths and answer values were excluded from the public collection. No first-person claim of independently discovering a vulnerability was inherited from a source note.

General recommendations were checked against the linked OWASP, NIST and Apache documentation. They are educational analysis, not findings against a production organisation. No CVSS score or current zero-day claim is assigned.

The separate portfolio labs have their own source code and execution evidence. Links to them show related concepts; they do not prove how a TryHackMe room was solved.

## Publication rule

The [TryHackMe Acceptable Use Policy](https://tryhackme.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy), version 2.0 effective 28 August 2026, restricts sharing solutions for active content and permits retired-content writeups under its guidance. No retired designation was verified for the eight rooms. Accordingly this collection is limited to general learning retrospectives and excludes solution content. Certification and examination material is outside this collection.

## Updating a report

Changes should preserve this distinction between completion evidence, reference material, original analysis and executed tests. If an error is found, identify the specific report and passage; update the technical explanation and its source together. Add further rooms only after checking completion and usable notes. A full walkthrough requires confirmed publication eligibility and traceable source authorship.
